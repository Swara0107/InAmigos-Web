Who We Are

Driven by Compassion,
Powered by Community

🇮🇳 Section 8 Registered · Pan-India

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered nonprofit organization founded in 2020, working across India to create positive social change through impactful community initiatives.

We believe that every individual deserves dignity, opportunity, and a safe environment. Our passionate team of volunteers and interns work tirelessly across 28 states to make this a reality.
